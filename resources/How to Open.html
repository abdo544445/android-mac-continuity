Opening Continuity Companion

Quick 3-step guide for macOS Sonoma & Sequoia

1
Drag ContinuityCompanion.app into your Applications folder and double-click to launch it.
2
When macOS displays the "Not Opened" Gatekeeper dialog, click Done.
(Do NOT click Move to Trash)
3
Click the button below to jump straight to Privacy & Security settings, scroll to the bottom, and click Open Anyway.
Open Privacy & Security Settings
This one-time confirmation is standard for independent macOS applications.
Continuity Companion runs 100% locally with zero cloud telemetry.
